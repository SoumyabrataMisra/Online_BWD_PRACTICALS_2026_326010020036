About Marwadi University

Marwadi University is a higher-education institution in Rajkot, Gujarat, focused on academic excellence, innovation, research and holistic student development.

The university was established under the Gujarat Private Universities Act No. 9 of 2016 and promotes critical thinking, creativity and skills for future careers.

Purpose of Using an Iframe

An iframe (Inline Frame) allows an external webpage or service to be embedded inside the current HTML page. In this project, the iframe displays the Google Maps location of Marwadi University.

Using an iframe is useful because the webpage does not need to build its own map system. It can safely present external content such as maps, videos, forms or other web resources within a defined area.
