About the Department

The Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) department at Marwadi University provides quality education in computer applications, programming and information technology. The Online BCA programme helps students develop technical knowledge and practical skills.

Vision

To provide quality education in computer applications and develop skilled, innovative and responsible professionals who can contribute to the digital world.

Mission

  • To provide quality education in computer applications.
  • To develop programming and problem-solving skills.
  • To encourage creativity and innovation.
  • To prepare students for professional careers in IT.
  • To promote lifelong learning and ethical values.
