ABOUT THE UNIVERSITY

Education for a Changing World

Marwadi University is a higher education institution in Rajkot, Gujarat. The university focuses on empowering students through academic learning, innovation, research and practical development.

Its academic offerings include undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, research and online programs across several disciplines.
